our story

A Hearth That Never Went Cold

It began as one coal angithi in the corner of a Chandni Chowk courtyard, where our grandmother cooked dal through the night so it would be ready by the time the family woke. There was no restaurant then — only a fire that was never allowed to go out, and a rule that nobody left the table hungry.

Three generations later, the rule hasn't changed. We still hand-grind our masalas every morning, still let the dal simmer for twelve hours over coal, and still write new recipes into the same battered notebook our grandmother started. We just built a dining room around the fire, and set a few more places at the table. We remain proudly 100% vegetarian.

"We don't cook to impress. We cook the way we'd feed our own family — and then we set a place for you." — Kamla Rawat, Founder
